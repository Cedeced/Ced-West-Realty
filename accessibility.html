Accessibility statement

Last updated October 27, 2026

Cedrick J. Grizzard, Real Estate Agent with Pinnacle Estate Properties Inc., wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use this website and get help with buying, selling, investing, or leasing a home.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA (opens in new tab), the widely used standard for accessible websites.

What we do

  • Pages work with a keyboard alone, show where the keyboard focus is, and include a “Skip to main content” link.
  • Pages are organized with headings and labeled regions so screen readers can move through them easily.
  • Images have text descriptions, and decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text meets color-contrast guidelines and stays readable when zoomed to 400% or viewed on a phone.
  • Every form field has a visible label, and form messages are announced to screen readers.
  • Links that open in a new tab say so to screen readers.
  • Animations are turned off for visitors whose device is set to reduce motion.

Third-party services

Some features rely on outside services: the hCaptcha spam check on our forms, Calendly for booking a consultation, and Google Forms for The Grizzlist questionnaire. We chose services that publish their own accessibility commitments, but we don’t control them. If any of them gets in your way, contact us using any method below and we’ll help you directly.

Need help or found a barrier?

If you have trouble using any part of this site, or need information in another format, please tell us. We’ll respond within two business days and work with you to provide what you need.
